This repository's original procedural models, their authoring source, and the
project-authored narration text are dedicated to the public domain under the
Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal waiver:

https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Provenance: created specifically for Inside Socket Chat. The model source uses
Three.js primitives and the Three.js GLTFExporter; it does not include external
mesh or texture assets.

The generated MP3 previews are synthesized locally from that narration text
using the installed Microsoft Zira Desktop System.Speech voice. They are marked
provisional in the audio manifest and are not included in the CC0 dedication.
Confirm the installed voice's terms before redistributing the MP3 files.
